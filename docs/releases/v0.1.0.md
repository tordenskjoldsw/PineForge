# PineForge v0.1.0 — Experimental OTA Proof of Concept

> [!CAUTION]
> This is experimental proof-of-concept firmware, not a production release.
> It may require rollback or recovery and could leave a sealed PineTime
> unusable. Keep an official InfiniTime DFU package available and read the
> installation and recovery instructions before flashing.

PineForge v0.1.0 is the first hardware-tested Rust/Embassy proof of concept
with a complete Gadgetbridge-compatible OTA path. On a real sealed PineTime,
an OTA transfer from PineForge reached 100%, validated the staged image,
rebooted through MCUBoot, and successfully returned to InfiniTime.

## Highlights

- Rust 2024 firmware built on Embassy for the nRF52832
- ST7789 display and CST816S touch support
- BMA421 motion and HRS3300 heart-rate integration
- persistent settings and BLE bond storage
- InfiniTime-compatible pairing and phone time synchronization
- Nordic Legacy DFU service accepted by Gadgetbridge
- negotiated 251-byte ATT MTU payload support
- pipelined external-flash writes with packet-receipt synchronization
- on-watch DFU progress and concrete flash-failure diagnostics
- MCUBoot image confirmation, activation, rollback, and recovery paths
- support for XTX XT25F32B and Boya BY25Q32 external flash variants

## Hardware validation

Validated in the tested setup:

1. PineForge booted through the existing InfiniTime MCUBoot bootloader.
2. Gadgetbridge transferred an OTA package to PineForge.
3. The watch reached 100% without the former end-of-transfer deadlock.
4. The image was validated and activated.
5. MCUBoot rebooted into the staged InfiniTime image.

Hardware behavior can vary with PineTime revision, flash chip, bootloader,
Android device, and Gadgetbridge version.

## Known limitations

- OTA transfer speed is slower than stock InfiniTime.
- PineForge remains incomplete and is intended for development and hardware
  testing.
- The release has not been validated across all PineTime hardware or
  bootloader revisions.
- Recovery from a failed update may be difficult on a sealed watch.

## Installation

1. Fully charge the watch.
2. Save a known-good official InfiniTime DFU ZIP on the phone.
3. Read `GETTING-STARTED.md` and the sealed-device testing guide.
4. Download `pineforge-mcuboot-app-dfu-0.1.0.zip` from this release.
5. Verify its SHA-256 checksum against the value published with the release.
6. Open the ZIP with Gadgetbridge's firmware installer.
7. Keep the phone and watch close together until validation and reboot finish.

Do not extract the DFU ZIP before giving it to Gadgetbridge.

## Recovery

- Before PineForge is confirmed, an ordinary reset lets MCUBoot roll back to
  the previous InfiniTime image.
- To start the bootloader's minimal InfiniTime recovery image, hold the
  physical side button during boot until the boot logo turns red.
- Connect to that recovery image over Bluetooth and install a known-good
  InfiniTime or PineForge DFU ZIP.
